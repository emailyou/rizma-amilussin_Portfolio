About Me

My name is Rizma Amilussin, and I am from Naga. I am 18 years old, born on May 22, 2008. I am currently a first-year college student taking up BSBA major in Financial Management. Before entering college, I used to feel afraid and nervous about this new stage of my life. However, I am now a college student, and I am learning how to become more independent, responsible, and confident.

At present, I live in a dormitory while pursuing my studies. Living away from home has taught me many valuable lessons, especially about independence, time management, and responsibility. I have to manage my daily needs while balancing my academic responsibilities. I also do my schoolwork and other tasks independently, which helps me become more disciplined and resourceful.

I consider myself a hardworking student who wants to improve and learn new things. I believe that every challenge is an opportunity to grow and become a better person. Although I sometimes experience difficulties and feel uncertain about the future, I remind myself of my dreams and the reason I continue studying. My family is one of my biggest motivations, and I want to make them proud through my efforts and achievements.

I know that I still have a lot to learn, especially as I adjust to college life and my chosen course. Nevertheless, I am willing to do my best, develop my abilities, and continue moving forward. I believe that with determination, patience, faith, and hard work, I can achieve my goals and build a better future for myself and my family.
